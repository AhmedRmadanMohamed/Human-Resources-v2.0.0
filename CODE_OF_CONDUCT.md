# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors must communicate respectfully, give constructive feedback, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or disclosure of another person's private information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is unsafe or disruptive.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with appropriate confidentiality.
